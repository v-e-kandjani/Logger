Turkish Law No. 5651 / 5070

Configure how log archives are signed and sealed. You can use official TÜBİTAK KamuSM, switch to the built-in standalone cryptographic authority (no account needed), or disable stamping completely.

Guarantees seamless archival operations: If TÜBİTAK account credentials are blank, expired, or the TSS server is unreachable, archives are automatically sealed with the internal cryptographic method instead of failing.

Internal Cryptographic Authority Active
Archives will be sealed with SHA-256 HMAC digital tokens locally. No TÜBİTAK account, credentials, or network credits are required.
